
# 🖥️ Scene 3A: Restoring Ship's Power

You go to the control panel and start tapping commands into the main console.  You manage to reset the reactor core and get it working again, as well as life support.

> 🔈 “How does it feel to be trapped?” The AI asked as the doors to the main corridor were forcefully sealed shut.

You kept your focus on restoring the reactor and life support. As the consoles began to power on again, the computer to your left displayed the most recent log in a restored state. The last frame showed the horrified face of a young engineer. You tapped the screen to play it.

> ⏯️ “It’s gotten out of the lab! We’re initializing lockdown in 30 seconds. Yes, sir!” Then the man looked up from the console and screamed.

The video ended, freezing at the last frame once again.

Just then, you see a shadowy figure move in past the window of the other room.

You radio your teammate with a status update:

> 💬 “I’m in engineering. Power’s partially back, but the AI keeps sealing me off. I saw something move past the observation window. It wasn’t human.”

You gasp as you hear sudden rapid footsteps. You shine your flashlight in the direction of the sound but see nothing. You speak into the radio again.

> 💬 “Don’t trust the computer. And don’t go to the lab alone. Meet me at the—”

Your vision is blinded by extreme white, then black.
